FAQ

Can I book if I don't have a passport yet?

Yes — select "No (I'm working on it)" and we'll share deadlines and help you stay on track.

Is this tailored for ages 17–30 only?

That's our focus for group dynamic, but you can still request a booking. We'll confirm fit and logistics.

How do roommates work?

Tell us your preference in the notes. We try to match roommates by preference and availability.

What happens after I submit this form?

We contact you (WhatsApp/phone/email) to confirm package details, collect your $500 deposit, and send the agreement to sign.
